## Objective

Measure whether a policy improves actual FPL decisions over a no-intervention control, not merely whether its player point forecasts have lower error.

The three required policies are:
- hold
- conventional
- cta_informed

All three begin from the exact same manager state at the first deadline. After that, each policy's squad, bank, free transfers, chip inventory and selected lineup evolve independently. Do not reset later policies to the manager's actual subsequent squad: that would turn the experiment back into one-step replay rather than a sequential counterfactual.

## Per-manager input bundle

A private JSON object contains:
- manager ID and frozen sampling weight;
- exact initial manager state, including deadline, bank, free transfers, chip inventory, 15-player squad and selected lineup;
- ordered Gameweek rows;
- for each Gameweek, one shared official outcome map (official points, minutes and played status);
- for every policy, a frozen action record;
- for every non-terminal Gameweek, the next deadline and shared next-deadline player-price/team snapshot.

Each policy action record contains:
- starting_state_sha256: hash of the exact policy-specific state before the action;
- forecast_frozen_at_utc and forecast_sha256 for conventional and CTA-informed policies;
- decision_frozen_at_utc;
- action: transfers, starting XI, ordered bench, captain, vice-captain and optional chip.

The runner requires a timezone-aware forecast and decision time strictly before the deadline, with the forecast frozen no later than the decision. It verifies the state hash before applying each action. Conventional and CTA-informed actions require a valid SHA-256 forecast reference.

## Hold control

Hold is a genuine no-intervention control:
- zero transfers;
- no chip;
- preserve the existing starting XI, bench order, captain and vice-captain.

The runner rejects a Hold action that changes any of these. The prior lineup is carried forward in state transitions. Free transfers roll over normally, and the squad's market prices are refreshed at the next deadline.

## Sequential execution

For each policy and Gameweek:
1. Verify that the policy state is at the expected Gameweek and deadline.
2. Verify the frozen decision/forecast metadata and starting-state hash.
3. Apply the action through the deterministic state-transition engine. This validates roster, formation, budget, transfer prices, club limit, transfer hits and chip inventory.
4. Score the resulting Gameweek squad against the shared official points/minutes/played outcomes through the one-Gameweek replay engine.
5. Carry the policy's own next state forward. Free Hit restores the permanent squad, bank and prior permanent lineup; Wildcard changes persist.
6. The final Gameweek uses terminal mode. No artificial GW39 deadline or price snapshot is created.

The runner reports per-policy gross points, net points, transfer hits, transfers and Gameweek results, plus paired cumulative net-point deltas versus Hold. It does not use forecast MAE/RMSE as a substitute for decision value.

## Aggregate and sampling rules

The panel manifest supplies the original normalized design weights. The aggregator:
- verifies that each trajectory belongs to the frozen panel and carries its exact manifest weight;
- reports unweighted and design-weighted means for captured managers;
- reports paired policy deltas and positive/negative/tie rates;
- reports captured and missing design-weight shares;
- does not renormalize missing managers into a full-population claim.

If any panel weight is missing, the result is explicitly CAPTURED_SUBSET_ONLY. Weighted means are conditional on the captured weight mass, not estimates for the full selected population. Do not interpret them as population estimates without resolving or bounding missingness.

## Deliberate exclusions

- No live FPL recommendations are generated by this replay runner.
- The runner does not choose transfers, lineups, captaincy or chips. It executes frozen actions from the policy-generation layer.
- It does not calculate rank gain, effective ownership, field-relative outcomes or CoVar. Those are downstream evaluation layers and must not be silently folded into the point scorer.
- It does not calculate statistical uncertainty yet. The next statistical step is paired bootstrap uncertainty clustered by manager trajectory, preserving the original sampling design and missing-weight caveat.
- No real policy action bundles have been frozen or replayed yet; the current code/CI results validate the replay infrastructure only.

5. Run the replay on the captured panel and disclose missing-weight coverage.
6. Add manager-clustered paired uncertainty and then field-relative rank/EO/CoVar evaluation as separate reports.
